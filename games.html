Arcade

Seven browser games: six built with plain HTML5 Canvas, plus id Software’s 1993 DOOM via js-dos. No frameworks, just like the good old days, except everything is green and vaguely threatening.

  • PACKET_SNAKE

    Controls: Arrow keys / WASD

    Eat the network packets before they time out. Each packet consumed increases your length and score. Don’t run into the walls or yourself.

    Score 0High 0

  • HEX_BREACH

    Controls: Type to neutralise

    Malicious hex payloads are raining down. Type them exactly to neutralise each one before it reaches the kernel. You have 3 lives.

    LivesScore 0

  • FIREWALL_BREAKOUT

    Controls: Mouse / ← →

    Your packet has to punch through five layers of firewall rules. Deflect it with the paddle; drop it three times and the connection is dead.

    Score 0High 0

  • PORT_INVADERS

    Controls: ← → · Space

    Waves of malware processes are descending on your open ports. Shoot them down before they reach the perimeter, and dodge their payloads.

    Score 0High 0

  • LOGIC_BOMB_SWEEPER

    Controls: Right-click or hold to flag

    14 logic bombs are hidden in the memory grid. Clear every safe address; the numbers tell you how many bombs sit in adjacent cells. Flag what you can’t touch.

    Bombs 14Best —

  • MEMORY_LEAK

    Controls: Repeat the sequence

    The heap is leaking addresses in a pattern. Watch the sequence, then repeat it exactly. One wrong read and it’s a segfault. How deep can you go?

    Round 0Best —

    Controls: Keyboard · 1993 shareware
